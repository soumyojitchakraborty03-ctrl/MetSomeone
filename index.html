Pick your kind of table.

Every weekend there's a mix of themed tables and formats beyond cafés, so there's always one that fits your mood.

Café tables

A small group, about two hours, at a partner café picked for good food and a noise level that lets you actually talk.

Themed tables

Film nerds, startup people, readers, adda lovers, new-in-Kolkata. Start with something in common and go from there.

Dating tables

Singles-only tables matched for romantic compatibility. A relaxed group, not a job interview over coffee.

Walks & board games

Heritage walks through north Kolkata, riverside strolls by the Hooghly and board-game afternoons, for people who talk better while doing something.

Workshops

Pottery, sketching and other hands-on sessions in small groups. Leave with something you made and people you met.

Big mixers & repeat groups

Every so often all the tables meet at a 20+ mixer. Loved your table? Book the same group again next weekend.

Safe by design, not as an afterthought.

  • ID check for every member

    Everyone is verified before their first booking. No anonymous profiles.

  • Women-only tables

  • Public places only

    Partner cafés and open public spaces. Never someone's home.

  • Your number stays yours

    Contacts are shared only when both people say yes after the session.
